About Rajasthan

Rajasthan is the largest state of India by an area 3,42,239 sq km. It encompasses most of the area of the large, inhospitable Great Indian Desert (Thar Desert), which has an edge paralleling the Sutlej-Indus River valley along its border with Pakistan. The state has borders with Pakistan to the west, Gujrat to the southwest, Madhya Pradesh to the southeast, Uttar Pradesh & Haryana to the northeast & Punjab to the north. The glory of the state is retained by its majestic places, forts, monuments, vibrant culture, and rich heritage which draw innumerable tourists from across the globe.

About Jaipur

Jaipur, the Pink City of India, is an enormously admired tourist destination in Rajasthan and India. As per the census of 2011 & 2008, it is the 10 th largest city of India ranking among the fifty engineering global outsourcing cities. It was founded on 18th November, 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, the ruler of Amber. The city is colourful with oscillation of organized architecture unveiling the royal culture, arts, and traditions as the heritage of Rajasthan.

About Poornima University

Poornima University (PU) is a state private University in Jaipur, India established by an act of the Rajasthan legislature. PU was established in 2012 with a vision to create a knowledge- based society with scientific temper, team spirit and dignity of labour to face global competition and challenges. PU is recognized by University Grants Commission (UGC) under Section 2(f) and 12(B) with the right to confer degrees as per Section 22 (1) of the UGC Act, 1956. Its architecture program is approved by the Council of Architecture (CoA) and the hotel management program is approved by NCHMCT. Currently, PU has more than 8000+ students from all over the country enrolled in nine faculties and fourteen departments of UG/PG and PhD Programs in various areas such as Management, Engineering, Computer Application Sciences, Architecture, Design, Public Health and Hotel Management etc.

About FSH

Established in the year 2017, the Faculty of Science and Humanities (FSH) stands as a dynamic and multidisciplinary academic division dedicated to fostering intellectual growth, scientific inquiry, and holistic development. Since its inception, the Faculty has grown steadily, offering a diverse array of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes tailored to meet contemporary academic and societal demands.

FSH initially commenced with foundational programmes such as the Bachelor of Science in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (B.Sc. PCM), Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), and Bachelor of Science in Environmental Science (B.Sc. Environment). Over the years, the Faculty has broadened its academic horizons by introducing additional undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, reflecting an ongoing commitment to academic excellence and relevance. A significant hallmark of the Faculty is its vibrant academic community comprising more than 25 qualified and experienced faculty members. These educators are dedicated not only to teaching but also to research, innovation, and mentorship, thereby enriching the academic environment and contributing to scholarly discourse across disciplines. In alignment with national educational goals and the evolving landscape of higher education, FSH has also initiated postgraduate and doctoral programmes. Notably, the Faculty offers a Master of Arts in Yoga (M.A. Yoga), which combines traditional knowledge systems with modern therapeutic approaches, promoting wellness and holistic education. Furthermore, the introduction of Ph.D. programmes across various disciplines underscores the Faculty’s role as a centre for advanced research and academic leadership. Through its interdisciplinary approach, student-centric pedagogy, and commitment to values-based education, the Faculty of Science and Humanities continues to serve as a cornerstone of academic excellence within the institution.

About the R&D Cell
