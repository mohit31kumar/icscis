Call for Papers

The research areas include, but are not limited to:

Track 1: Cultural and Social Dimensions of Food

Food, Trauma and Narrative Healing Cuisine Cultures: (Re)Construing Meanings and Metaphors Folk /Oral Responses to Food across Histories and Cultures “I am What I Eat”: Psychological Approaches to Food Food Aesthetics: A Philosophical Examination of Culinary Studies
Food, Migration and Memory Gendered Representations of Food and Culinary Practices Cultures and Narratives of Food in Texts/Media Women in Food Production, Preparation, and Preservation Food and Festivals: Cultural and Religious Perspectives

Track 2: Food, Nutrition and Healthcare Practices

Nutrigenomics and personalized nutrition Functional foods and dietary interventions Food safety and public health challenges Traditional dietary practices vs. modern nutrition The science of taste and sensory evaluation
Gendered access to food and nutritional disparities Food labour, domesticity, and the politics of care

Track 3: From Production to Consumption: Food, Environment and Sustainability

Sustainable Agriculture Production Environment and Systems of Food Production Consumption Patterns of Food and Consumer Behaviour Sustainable Food Policy and International Trade Food Sovereignty and Local vs. Global Food Systems
Food Studies and Ecological Approaches

Track 4: Flavours of Hospitality and Strategic Approaches to Food

Culinary Tourism and Cultural Identity Fusion Cuisine and Global Influences Health and Wellness Trends in Menu Design Digital Innovations in Food and Hospitality Food Cultures and Global Hospitality

Track 5: Technological Innovations and the Future of Food Sub-themes

Biotechnology and genetically modified organisms (GMOs) AI and big data in food production and distribution Alternative proteins: plant-based, insect-based, and cultured meat Smart packaging and food traceability Urban farming, vertical agriculture, and hydroponics

NOTE: Session wise " Best Paper Award " along with certificates to all the authors.
