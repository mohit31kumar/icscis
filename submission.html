Author Guidelines

We aim to offer a fulfilling and enriching experience to all contributing authors through:

  • The Abstract should be between 300-350 words in Times New Roman 12, regular and single line.
  • The Abstract must indicate: Title of paper, name(s) of author(s), affiliation(s), and contact details, purpose of study, research methodology, results, managerial implications, and key references and keywords.
  • Author must clearly indicate the track under which the submission is done.
  • Submitted abstracts and full papers would undergo blind review by expert reviewers.
  • Authors of accepted papers, who wish to publish their accepted papers in journals with the conference tie-up, will need to submit the full paper as per the guidelines of the journal.
  • All submissions must be original and must not be currently under consideration or accepted for publication or presentation elsewhere.

Submission Guidelines:

  • Title of the paper: bold, title case (Capitalize each word), centered.
  • Text of the paper: justified. Font & size: Times New Roman 12, Spacing: 1.5, Margin of 1 inch on all four sides
  • References: MLA style (9th ed.) strictly, such as:
    • Don’t use Foot Notes. Instead use End Notes
    • Titles of books: Italics
    • Titles of articles from journals and books: “quoted”
    • In-text short citation (" " double quotes)
    • In-text long citation (Indent as per MLA)
  • Articles should be submitted as MS Word 2003-2007 attachments only
  • Word Limit 5000-6000 words
  • Full paper must be accompanied by:
    • A declaration that it is an original work and has not been published/sent anywhere else for publication
    • A short bio-note of the contributor(s) indicating name, institutional affiliation, brief academic/career history (50-100 words)
    • A plagiarism report, (Kindly get it checked through the software that detects the plagiarism index of the AI generated content also).

Where to Submit:

Submission Portal: Papers must be submitted in word format through CMT.

How to Submit:

  1. Prepare your manuscript as per conference template (In word format)
  2. Create an account on CMT
  3. Submit your manuscript
    1. Log in to the submission portal.
    2. Click on "New Submission"
    3. Fill in the required details:
      • Title of the paper
      • Abstract (200–250 words)
      • Author names (note: these are not included in the uploaded paper but required in metadata)
      • Keywords (5–6)
    4. Upload your manuscript as a Word file.
    5. Confirm that the submission is anonymous and plagiarism-free.
    6. Click Submit.

Paper Structure:

Each submission should include the following key components:

  1. Title: Concise and indicative of the paper's contribution.
  2. Abstract:
    • Length: 200–250 words
    • Should clearly state the objective, methodology, key results, and conclusion.
  3. Keywords: 5–6 relevant keywords
  4. Main Body: Clearly divided into Introduction, Related Work, Methodology, Results, Discussion, Conclusion.
  5. Figures & Tables: Must be numbered, high resolution, and referenced in text.
  6. References:
    • Follow citation style
    • Use citation management tools like EndNote, Mendeley, or Zotero
    • Must include recent and relevant works (avoid self-citation abuse)

Author Expectations & Attendance:

  • At least one author per paper must register and attend the conference to present their work.
  • Presentations can be oral (in-person or virtual, based on availability).
  • Authors are expected to prepare a 10 minute PowerPoint presentation.
  • No-show papers will not be considered for publication.
  • Nobody will be allowed to make more than two presentations during the conference. This is done to ensure diversity and inclusivity of more active participants in the conference.

Publication Details:

  • All accepted and presented papers will be submitted to Peter Lang(confirmed) or scopus-indexed(to be confirmed) conference proceedings for publication.

Review Process:

Each submission will undergo at least two rounds of peer review:

  1. Initial Screening: Scope, format, and plagiarism check.
  2. Technical Review: By two or more subject experts (double-blind).

Review criteria include: Novelty and originality, Technical soundness, Clarity and organization, Relevance to the conference themes.

All papers with a similarity score of less than 10% will undergo a double-blind review. All submissions will be screened using Turnitin. The technical committee of the conference will review the papers, and the authors will be notified of the paper status, which can be one of the following:

  1. Accepted for oral presentation
  2. Accepted with minor corrections for Oral Presentation
  3. Rejected

Additional Guidelines:

  • Camera-Ready Submission: Must include author details and comply with formatting rules.
  • Language Check: Authors are encouraged to proofread or use language editing services before submission.
  • Multiple Submissions: Authors may submit multiple papers but must register separately for each accepted paper.
  • Certificates: Authors will receive certificates for Presentation and Publication.

Revision and Camera-Ready:

If accepted, authors will receive reviewer comments and are required to submit a revised camera-ready version in accordance with the publisher's guidelines.

Final manuscript must include:

  • All author details
  • Conflict of interest declaration (if required)
  • Funding acknowledgment (if applicable)
