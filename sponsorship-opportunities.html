Sponsorship Opportunities for ICSCIS-2025

Sponsorship of ICSCIS-2025 will provide a company or an organization with a unique opportunity to increase its visibility and product awareness within a highly specialized, influential community.

Diamond Sponsorship (Rs. 30,000)

  • Three full conference registrations free of cost
  • Three stalls space for exhibits
  • Inclusion of your company's name and logo in our web advertising on ICSCIS-2025 site, linkage to your company website
  • Acknowledgment in opening address

Gold Sponsorship (Rs. 20,000)

  • Two full conference registrations free of cost
  • Two stalls space for exhibits
  • Inclusion of your company's name and logo in our web advertising on ICSCIS-2025 site, linkage to your company website
  • Acknowledgment in opening address

Bronze Sponsorship (Rs. 10,000)

  • One full conference registration free of cost
  • One stall space for exhibits
  • Inclusion of your company's name and logo in our web advertising on ICSCIS-2025 site, linkage to your company website
  • Acknowledgment in opening address
